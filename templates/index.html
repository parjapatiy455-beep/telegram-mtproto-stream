How to Stream on Any Website

Use standard HTML5 video tag or iframe embed in your website:

<!-- Option 1: Responsive Iframe -->
<iframe 
  src="{{ base_url }}/embed/123" 
  width="100%" 
  height="450" 
  frameborder="0" 
  allowfullscreen>
</iframe>

<!-- Option 2: HTML5 Video Tag -->
<video controls width="100%">
  <source src="{{ base_url }}/stream/123/video.mp4" type="video/mp4">
</video>
